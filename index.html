Projects

The Interpreter’s Dilemma

Live · Oct 2026 to present

A browser game that puts you in the interpreter’s seat during a Turkish–English business call. Drawing on my work interpreting for Islamic traditional arts programs, each scene tests a real decision: a specialist term, an idiom, a vague time, or a question that tests neutrality. After every choice, the game shows what the other person actually understood.

  • Six scored scenes covering accuracy, terminology, neutrality, and transparency
  • A “meaning kept vs. lost” visualization and a trust meter
  • Turkish–English content, with interpretation in both directions
  • Light and dark themes, a responsive layout, and keyboard accessibility

Play the game

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, no dependencies
